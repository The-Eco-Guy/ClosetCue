Welcome to ClosetCue's Wardrobe Organization Guide!

Step 1: Sort your clothes by category — Tops, Bottoms, One-Pieces, and Footwear.

Step 2: Within each category, group by occasion — Casual, Formal, Party, and Festive.

Step 3: Note the shelf location for each group so you can find items quickly.

Step 4: Add each item to ClosetCue with a photo and its shelf location.

Now you can browse, filter, and pick outfits without messing up your shelves!
